# Artes inéditas da jornada do perfil

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada image_gen, com fundo transparente. As artes do
onboarding serviram somente como referência de personagem e linguagem visual.
Arquivos finais: public/profile-journey/helena-{identity,avatar,cover,complete}.webp.
O script prepare-profile-journey-art.mjs apenas reduz e comprime, preservando alfa.

## Prompts finais

### identity

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent mascot cutout for OlenaStudy profile-completion card. Input images 1 and 2 are STRICT CHARACTER AND PAPER-CUT STYLE REFERENCES ONLY, not poses to copy. Create ONE NEW pose of the SAME Helena cat: charcoal/purple-black angular polygon paper facets, huge yellow polygon eyes with dark pupils and tiny cream highlights, pointed ears, yellow five-point star attached to her left ear (viewer right), small compact body, polygon paws and curled tail. Match original proportions and flat layered cut-paper medium, not plush, furry, glossy 3D, human or smooth cartoon. Full body and prop entirely visible, centered with generous transparent padding. True transparent background, no cream rectangle, no scene, no frame, no text, no watermark. All limbs anatomically coherent, exactly two forepaws and two hind paws. NEW POSE IDENTITY: Helena stands in a curious three-quarter stance and proudly presents a small cream-and-purple identity badge in both forepaws at chest level. Badge has a simple purple silhouette and one yellow star, no letters. Head tilted inquisitively, ears alert, tail curling behind. Distinct from holding a book or pencil. Friendly 'Who are you?' introduction.
