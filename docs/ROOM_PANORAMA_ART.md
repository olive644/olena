## Prompts de produção

Escuta:

Edit this original application papercraft illustration into a wide panoramic 3:1 button banner. Preserve Helena exactly: black faceted paper cat, yellow eyes, turquoise headphones, complete ears, paws and tail. Keep the original speaker, musical classroom and original solid paper-cut palette, facets and layered shadows. Fully show the cat and original important scene on the right half with safe margins. OUTPAINT the classroom to the left two thirds, creating quiet rich turquoise/navy paper wall facets for readable white UI text later. Seamless full-bleed illustration across the entire banner, no separate rectangle or inset image, no gradients, no photographic texture. No text, no letters, no button frame. High quality intentional original papercraft artwork.

Aplicado separadamente a cada uma das outras oito referências:

Outpaint this exact original application illustration into a panoramic 3:1 full-bleed button banner. Preserve the original character identity, all original paper-cut geometric solid colors and layered facets. Fully show the complete character, face, hands, feet and important bingo board or activity object in the RIGHT half with safe margins, no cropping. Expand the SAME matching classroom or solar-system environment seamlessly across the entire LEFT half with quiet dark teal/navy or purple paper wall/space facets, suitable for white UI text that will be overlaid separately. No separate inset rectangle, no small square panel, no gradients, no realistic 3D or plastic. Papercraft cartoon original app style, precise clean layered cuts. Preserve the illustration's specific action and bingo winning pattern. No words, letters, numbers, UI labels or frame.

## Preparação e revisão

WebP com 1200 pixels de largura, qualidade 86. Revisão conjunta das nove artes: personagens completos, padrões distintos nas cartelas, ambiente contínuo. Uso de `picture` evita cortar ou esticar personagens no celular. A indicação de seleção usa borda/base amarela e foco visível; a arte não depende de hover. As fontes e objetos principais das referências não foram sobrescritos.
