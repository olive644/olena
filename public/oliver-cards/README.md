# Oliver: as lembranças do sino

Dez ilustrações originais produzidas com o gerador de imagens integrado em 2026-10-10. Referências: notebook-covers/oliver-estrelas.webp (identidade), helena-onboarding-step-2.webp (material de papel), room-art/poliana-bingo-line.webp (cenários). O círculo do avatar não faz parte do personagem.

Cartas finais são HTML: título, moldura, atributos, história e verso Olena em src/components/oliver-card.tsx e oliver-card.css. Não há texto rasterizado. Atributos são ficcionais de coleção, sem bônus no minigame. As histórias próprias se conectam pelo sino, estrela, mapa e compasso, sem copiar outro universo.

Artes WebP de 480×720 e 720×1080, geradas como cenas completas 1024×1536. Redimensionamento sem recorte via ffmpeg, qualidades 72 e 75. Originais mantidos na pasta de imagens geradas do Codex, não no bundle.

Prompt-base: portrait 2:3, original conceptual illustration. Lock Oliver as round cyan/turquoise folded-paper mascot with pale cyan face, black heart eyes, wide smile, tiny mitten hands and feet. - first-star, A primeira estrela: Oliver at dawn finding a tiny folded gold star in a shallow turquoise stream. Close low angle, round face dominant, wonder and discovery. Cobalt river stones, ivory morning sky, one gold reflection.
- silent-bell, Sino sem eco: Oliver beside a small old ivory bell tower with a gold bell whose tiny clapper is missing. Oliver listens with one mitten hand at the bell, calm melancholy. Blue dusk sky and stepped cream stones, framing from below.
- chalk-map, Mapa de giz: Oliver unrolling a parchment map covered in non-readable geometric paths at a stone table. A blue triangle shaped hole in the map. Top-down three quarter view, mint and sky-blue light, gold compass nearby.
- split-compass, Compasso partido: Oliver carefully holding two halves of a golden drafting compass in his turquoise mitten hands, his face tenderly smiling. Tight portrait, ivory workshop with cobalt shelves, warm gold focused center, clear star scratch on compass.
- third-bridge, A terceira ponte: Oliver standing on the complete gold protractor bridge of the original mathematics world over a blue canyon, carrying a folded star. Dynamic side view, wind pushes a small teal scarf. Cream towers and dramatic cobalt facets.
- glass-seed, Semente de vidro: Oliver kneeling to plant a single translucent geometric cyan seed inside a cracked ivory planter. Crystal sprout resembles a small gold star in center. Emerald polygon leaves on one side, compact gentle scene, low viewpoint.
- long-night, Vigília azul: Oliver sitting by a tiny gold lantern beneath a blue observatory roof, watching a far gold bell tower silhouette. Quiet night, navy polygon sky with a few angular gold stars. Character round and recognizable not human body, medium shot.
- river-memory, Memória do rio: Oliver crossing cream stepping stones on a turquoise canal, a folded gold paper boat carries a tiny bell clapper nearby. Reflections made of flat colored facets. Dynamic diagonal composition, aqua gold ivory, hands reaching for boat.
- last-note, A última nota: Oliver in the old cream bell tower holding the recovered tiny gold clapper, a folded gold star hovering over the bell. Joyful circular face, heart eyes, bright warm dawn light and blue geometric windows. Upward close viewpoint.
- homeward, Caminho de volta: Oliver walking toward a cozy cobalt-roofed ivory workshop, golden bell and planted crystal tree visible behind him in the same mathematics island. He holds a repaired gold compass, three quarter front view, sunset amber and turquoise, a small folded star in pocket.
